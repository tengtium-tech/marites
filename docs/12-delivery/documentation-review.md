# Documentation review

Version 0.3 | 10 November 2026 | Status: Reviewed; three review passes with mechanical checks. None is an independent review, and each was made by the author against the previous pass's conclusions rather than independently of them.

## Scope and method

The whole `docs/` set was read end to end three times: once at baseline 0.2 by the project author, once at baseline 0.4.1 in a completion pass that re-read every document and re-ran every check, and once on 10 November 2026 alongside the UI prototype — that pass found the audit-evidence gaps, because building the screen is what made them visible. Three mechanical checks were run before and after the fixes: every relative link resolves; every FR, NFR, ADR, DEC, AUD, ASM, RSK, TC, THR, OP and ERR identifier cited is defined; and both OpenAPI files validate as OpenAPI 3.1 — parsed and checked against the specification by `openapi-spec-validator`, with every `$ref` resolved, no operation untagged, and the operation counts matching the README.

Claims about RabbitMQ behaviour that could not be confirmed from the documents (the quorum-queue peek effect) are recorded as unverified rather than asserted. Where a finding needed a platform fact that this repository does not own, the finding states the contradiction rather than the platform's rule.

Findings carry AUD identifiers (registered in the [documentation standard](../01-project/documentation-standard.md)). Status is Resolved, Resolved as documented risk, or Withdrawn.

## What this review does not show

It does not establish that the design works. No code runs, no load test has been executed, and no behaviour has been observed against a broker. Two checks remain open by design: the capture load test (M2) and the quorum-queue peek test (M1). DEC-008's premise is still unconfirmed.

It also does not establish that the new documents are *right* — only that they exist, agree with the documents they cite, and resolve their identifiers. A threat model written by the same person who wrote the design is a consistency check, not an adversary.
